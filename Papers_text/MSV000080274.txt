=== TITLE ===
IPG strip-based peptide fractionation for shotgun proteomics.

=== ABSTRACT ===
Efficient fractionation of peptides is an essential prerequisite for comprehensive analysis of complex protein mixtures by shotgun mass spectrometry. The separation of peptides by isoelectric focusing is particularly attractive due to its orthogonality to reverse-phase HPLC. Here, we present a protocol for in-gel peptide isoelectric focusing using immobilized pH gradient strips. The method shows high resolving power for up to 1 mg of sample and is highly reproducible.

Peptide mixtures were separated by reversed-phase chromatogra- phy using the EASY-nLC on in-house-manufactured 20-cm fritless silica microcolumns with an inner diameter of 75 μm. Columns were packed with the ReproSil-Pur C18-AQ 3-μm resin. Peptides were separated on an 8–60 % acetonitrile gradient (214 min) with 0.1 % formic acid at a flow rate of 200 nl/min. Eluting peptides were ionized online by electrospray ionization and transferred into a Q-Exactive mass spectrometer. One full scan (m/z = 300–1,700; R = 70,000; target value = 3 × 106 ; maximum injection time = 20 ms) was followed by ten MS/MS scans (charge >1; R = 17,500; target value = 1 × 106 ; isolation window = 2 m/z; maximum injection time = 60 ms). Dynamic exclusion for selected precursor ions was 30 s. This method corresponds to a previously described “fast” acquisition method [13].
