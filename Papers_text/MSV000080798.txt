=== TITLE ===
Identification of a Novel Function of Adipocyte Plasma Membrane-Associated Protein (APMAP) in Gestational Diabetes Mellitus by Proteomic Analysis of Omental Adipose Tissue.

=== ABSTRACT ===
Gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) is considered as an early stage of type 2 diabetes mellitus. In this study, we compared demographic and clinical data between six GDM subjects and six normal glucose tolerance (NGT; healthy controls) subjects and found that homeostasis model of assessment for insulin resistance index (HOMA-IR) increased in GDM. Many previous studies demonstrated that omental adipose tissue dysfunction could induce insulin resistance. Thus, to investigate the cause of insulin resistance in GDM, we used label-free proteomics to identify differentially expressed proteins in omental adipose tissues from GDM and NGT subjects (data are available via ProteomeXchange with identifier PXD003095). A total of 3528 proteins were identified, including 66 significantly changed proteins. Adipocyte plasma membrane-associated protein (APMAP, a.k.a. C20orf3), one of the differentially expressed proteins, was down-regulated in GDM omental adipose tissues. Furthermore, mature 3T3-L1 adipocytes were used to simulate omental adipocytes. The inhibition of APMAP expression by RNAi impaired insulin signaling and activated NFκB signaling in these adipocytes. Our study revealed that the down-regulation of APMAP in omental adipose tissue may play an important role in insulin resistance in the pathophysiology of GDM.

=== METHODS ===
Subjects
Six GDM and six NGT subjects were enrolled in the study between July 2012 and January 2013. The diagnosis of GDM was based on International Association of Diabetes and Pregnancy Study Groups (IADPSG) criteria. (29) Omental adipose tissue samples were collected during caesarean deliveries. Each patient provided informed consent, the research was conducted in compliance with the declaration of Helsinki, and the protocol was approved by the ethics committees of Shanghai General Hospital, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. An omental adipose tissue sample (volume 1 to 2 cm3) was collected from each pregnant woman immediately after delivery during surgery. The adipose tissue sample was sliced into small pieces (∼5 mm3), washed with ice-cold PBS three times to completely remove the blood, transferred into 2 mL frozen tubes, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen for 5 min, and stored at −80 °C for proteomics analysis and Western blotting. (30) Tissue samples for immunohistochemistry (IHC) were fixed in a 4% formaldehyde solution and embedded in paraffin wax.
Demographic and Clinical Data Collection
Demographic data were collected, including age, height, weight, maximum weight during pregnancy, prepregnancy weight, parity, and gravidity. Clinical data were collected, including the results of the oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT), insulin release test, and serum lipid measurements (TCH, TG, LDL-C, and HDL-C) at 24–28 weeks of gestation. To evaluate β-cell function and insulin resistance, HOMA-β and HOMA-IR were calculated as HOMA-β = 20 × FINS (μU/mL)/(FPG [mmol/L] – 3.5) and HOMA-IR = FPG (mmol/L) × FINS (μU/mL)/22.5, respectively (31, 32)· The student’s t test was used to assess statistical significance between normally distributed data; the nonparametric Mann–Whitney U (Wilcoxon) test was used for non-normally distributed data (such as fasting serum insulin, 1 h postprandial serum insulin, 2 h postprandial serum insulin, HOMA-IR, and HOMA-β). Data are represented using mean ± SEM in Table 1, and the individual demographic and clinical data for all subjects are listed in Table S1. p value <0.05 was used as a cutoff criterion for a significant change.

Reagents
Ammonium bicarbonate, dithiothreitol (DTT), iodoacetamide (IAA), and sodium carbonate were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich (St. Louis, MO). Acetonitrile and water for nano-LC–MS/MS were purchased from J. T. Baker (Phillipsburg, NJ). Trypsin was purchased from Promega (Madison, WI). Strong cation exchange (SCX) beads were obtained from Polymer Laboratories, Agilent (Palo Alto, CA). CHAPS (3-[(3-cholamidopropyl) dimethylammonio]-1-propanesulfonate, BP571) and methanol were purchased from BioRad (Hercules, CA). Media and reagents used for cell culture studies were obtained from Invitrogen (Burlington, ON, Canada).
Protein Trypsin Digestion Using the FASP Method
Protein was extracted from adipose tissue using SDT lysis buffer (4% SDS, 0.1 M Tris-HCl and 0.1 M DTT, pH 7.8), and denatured by heating at 100 °C for 5 min. Insoluble material was removed by centrifugation at 16 000g, and the supernatant was kept for further analysis. Protein concentration was measured using a tryptophan-fluorescence assay as previously described. (33) Samples (50 μg each) were digested using the FASP procedure as previously described. (34, 35)
LC–MS/MS
Reverse-phase high-performance liquid chromatography (RP-HPLC) separation was performed with the EASY-nLC nano-LC system (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Bremen, Germany) using a self-packed column (75 μm × 150 mm; 3 μm ReproSil-Pur C18 beads, 120 Å, Dr. Maisch GmbH, Ammerbuch, Germany) at a flow rate of 300 nL/min. The RP–HPLC mobile phase A was 0.1% formic acid in water, and B was 0.1% formic acid in acetonitrile. Peptides were eluted over a 4 h period using a gradient (2–90% mobile phase B) and a nano-ESI Q-Exactive mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific, Bremen, Germany) operated in data-dependent mode with each full MS scan followed by MS/MS for the 20 highest-intensity ions with the following parameters: precursor ion charges ≥ +2, a precursor ion isolation window of 2 Da, and a normalized collision energy of 30 for HCD. Dynamic exclusion was set at 25 s. The full mass and subsequent MS/MS analyses were performed in an Orbitrap analyzer with R = 70 000 and 17 500 (at m/z 200), respectively.
Data Analysis
The MS data were analyzed using MaxQuant software (36) (http://maxquant.org/, version 1.3.0.5). Carbamidomethyl (C) was set as a fixed modification, and methionine oxidation (M, + 15.99492 Da) was set as a variable modification. Proteins were identified by searching the MS and MS/MS peptide data against a decoy version of the International Protein Index (IPI) human database (version 3.87, European Bioinformatics Institute). Trypsin/P was selected as the digestive enzyme with two potential missed cleavages. Label-free quantification was performed in MaxQuant as previously described. (36) Protein abundance was calculated on the basis of the normalized spectral protein intensity (LFQ intensity). The p value of Log2LFQ intensity of each protein between the GDM group and NGT group by two-tailed Student’s t test using the Perseus program, and p value <0.05 was set as a cutoff criterion for a significant change.
3T3-L1 Cell Culture, Differentiation, and siRNA Treatment
3T3-L1 preadipocytes were cultured in DMEM containing 25 mM glucose and 10% FBS at 37 °C in a 5% CO2 atmosphere. At 2 days postconfluence (day 0), differentiation into an adipocyte phenotype was induced by the addition of isobutylmethylxanthine (0.5 mmol/L), dexamethasone (1 μmol/L), and insulin (0.17 μmol/L) in DMEM containing 25 mmol/L glucose and 10% FBS. After 2 days, the isobutylmethylxanthine and dexamethasone were removed, and the cells were incubated in DMEM with insulin for an additional 2 days. On day 4, the medium was changed back to DMEM (without insulin supplementation) plus 10% FBS and was replenished every 2 days. (37) The cells were ready for use after 8 days of differentiation. Under these conditions, >95% of the cell population underwent morphologic differentiation into adipocytes. APMAP siRNA (sense 5′-GGCAGAGUCUUCAGAAUGATT-3′, antisense 5′-UCAUUCUGAAGACUCUGCCTT-3′ GenePharma) stock solution was prepared in RNase-free water in 20 μM concentration. Transfection experiment was performed by adding 2 mL of serum-free medium containing 100 nM APMAP siRNA and 0.5% (v/v) lipid carrier (Lipofectamine 3000, Invitrogen) to each well in the six-well dish. After 6 h, these transfected mature adipocytes were put back to DMEM cell culture medium supplemented with 10% FBS.
Western Blots
Equal amounts of proteins from mature 3T3-L1 adipocyte whole-cell lysates and omental adipose tissue samples were separated by sodium dodecyl sulfate polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE) and transferred to polyvinylidene difluoride membranes (Millipore Corporation, Bedford, MA). The membranes were blocked with 5% nonfat milk and incubated with primary antibodies overnight at 4 °C. Anti-APMAP [4F6] (ab119369) and anti-GAPDH antibodies were purchased from Abcam Company. Antibodies against insulin receptor substrate 1 (IRS-1), phosphatidylinositol 3 kinase (AKT), phospho (p)-AKT (Ser473), nuclear factor-k-gene binding (p65), p-p65 and inhibitor k binding α (IKβα) were purchased from Beyotime Biotechnology, and anti-p-IRS-1(Tyr612) was purchased from Cell Signaling Technology. The immunoblots were visualized with peroxidase-conjugated secondary antibodies (Sigma-Aldrich) using an ECL Western blotting analysis system. The Western blot data was normalized by the following formula: the gray value of protein X immunoblot/the gray value of GAPDH immnunoblot.
Immunohistochemistry
APMAP protein localization in omental adipose tissues was determined using immunohistochemistry (IHC). IHC staining was performed on formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded tissues sliced into 4 μm thick sections. Then, 3% H2O2 was added for 15 min at room temperature to block endogenous peroxidase. Following antigen recovery by microwave treatment, nonspecific binding was blocked with 10% goat serum. The sections were incubated with anti-APMAP ([4F6] (ab119369) 1:150, Abcam) at 4 °C overnight, washed in PBS, incubated with biotin-labeled secondary antibody at room temperature, stained with 3,3-diaminobenzidine (DAB), and counterstained with hematoxylin.
